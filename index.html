# Privacy Policy for Glow AI Skin App Last updated: March 2026 Glow AI Skin App ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. ## 1. Information We Collect ### Personal Information We may collect the following information: * Email address * User account information * Profile information ### Photos and Camera Data Our application allows users to upload selfies for AI-powered skin analysis. These images are used only to generate diagnostic results and are not shared publicly without your consent. ### Health and Skin Data We may collect information related to: * Skin type * Acne levels * Skin tone * Skin health scores * Lifestyle habits (sleep, diet, exercise) This data is used only to provide personalized skincare recommendations. ### Device Information We may collect: * Device type * Operating system * App usage statistics * Crash reports ### Location Data Location data may be used to analyze environmental factors such as pollution and UV exposure that may affect skin health. ## 2. How We Use Your Information We use collected information to: * Provide AI-powered skin analysis * Generate skincare recommendations * Improve app features and performance * Enable community features * Provide personalized lifestyle insights * Improve user experience ## 3. AI and Image Processing Photos uploaded for skin analysis are processed using artificial intelligence models to detect skin conditions such as acne, wrinkles, pores, and pigmentation. Images are processed securely and are not used for identity recognition or biometric identification. ## 4.
