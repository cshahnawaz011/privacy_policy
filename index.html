Privacy Policy for Skinaura Last Updated: May 9, 2026 Skinaura is a general beauty and wellness app that helps users track their skincare routines, try face yoga exercises, check cosmetic ingredients, and receive personalized beauty tips. We do not provide any medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and we do not collect any healthcare-related data such as medical reports, prescriptions, or clinical information. The information we collect includes basic account details (like your email address and username), profile preferences (such as skin type and beauty goals), photos you voluntarily upload for skin analysis or face yoga tracking, and anonymous usage data like device model and app feature interactions. We do not collect government IDs, precise GPS location, payment cards (Google Play handles all payments), or any sensitive health information. Your photos are processed locally on your device whenever possible and are not stored on our servers unless you explicitly save them. We use Google's Gemini API only for ingredient analysis, nutrition scanning, and face yoga feedback — no medical or diagnostic claims are made. Your data is stored on secure cloud servers in India and the United States with encryption in transit and at rest. We do not sell your personal information to anyone. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app settings. By using Skinaura, you agree to this privacy policy. If you have any questions, please contact us at support@skinaura.com.
